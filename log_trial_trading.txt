PURPOSE
This file is a durable project-memory record of the previous paper-trading trial and the defects found during post-trial audit. It must be read before changing trading-cycle, Market Pulse, agent-input, persistence, or dashboard-history behavior.

TRIAL OBSERVATIONS
1. Dashboard/history repeatedly represented the market as FLAT.
2. The paper bot produced HOLD/flat behavior throughout the previous trial as reported by the project owner.
3. Agent work was not sufficiently observable: the dashboard did not expose a complete per-cycle timeline, market timestamp, every agent result, candidate BUY/SELL signal, execution-gate result, and the exact market snapshot consumed by the agents.
4. The dashboard's main decision observation history was stored in browser localStorage, while the dedicated Paper History Library used Supabase. This created two different history sources.
5. The production deployment log showed that the React build succeeded and the Python package built successfully, but Cloudflare Worker validation failed while importing paper_cycle.py because line 1 contained the invalid statement `from __future__` instead of a complete future import.
6. The Cloudflare build log confirmed the Worker reached Wrangler deployment and exposed PAPER_STATE Durable Object and ASSETS bindings before validation failed.

IMPORTANT TRADING INTERPRETATION
HOLD is valid when the market has no executable edge. HOLD must NOT erase or hide an earlier/later directional candidate. If Forecast/Technical/Decision evidence identifies a prospective move after a flat regime, the candidate BUY/SELL signal must remain visible and be passed to the execution/risk gate. Only the gate may prevent execution; the observation must remain recorded.

MARKET DATA CONTRACT
Authoritative market source for the paper-trading cycle: INDODAX public market data.
The same market snapshot must be passed to every AI agent in a cycle.
Required market fields include:
- current price
- market timestamp
- recent public trades
- 1m/5m/15m/30m movement
- 1-minute OHLCV derived from public trades
- 30-minute Pulse status
- current 1-minute Pulse status
- 30-minute net movement
- data quality
- source identifier

MARKET PULSE CONTRACT
- A Pulse window covers 30 minutes.
- Within that 30-minute window, each minute is observable.
- A price change inside a minute must be represented; the system must not wait for the 30-minute window to close.
- GREEN = positive movement.
- RED = negative movement.
- GRAY = no valid movement/data for that minute or insufficient data for a directional classification.
- The overall 30-minute Pulse and the current minute Pulse are both persisted.

DO NOT REPEAT
- Do not classify the entire market as FLAT solely because a 30-minute regime is flat.
- Do not use browser localStorage as the authoritative trading history.
- Do not replace a candidate BUY/SELL with an unexplained HOLD without recording the execution-gate reason.
- Do not give different agents different market sources or timestamps in one cycle.
- Do not delete Supabase trial history before a recoverable archive/export exists.
- Do not declare Cloudflare deployment successful until Worker validation and a production smoke test pass.
