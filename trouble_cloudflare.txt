REFERENCE RESEARCH
------------------
Material UI's official installation documentation confirms React 18 is supported and that MUI Material is installed with Emotion peer dependencies. The published @mui/utils package exposes the relevant utility modules; therefore repeatedly patching individual missing exports is not an appropriate fix. MUI also documents legacy bundle aliases as a deliberate bundler option, not as a reason to rewrite package files during deployment.
